Hi! I'm **Leiser**, the traveller and photographer behind **Places, Not Faces**. I read every message, and I'm happy to hear from you.

# How to reach me

- **Email:** [hola@theplacesnotfaces.com](mailto:hola@theplacesnotfaces.com). The best way for anything detailed.
- **Instagram:** send a message to [@theplacesnotfaces](https://www.instagram.com/theplacesnotfaces/).
- **YouTube:** leave a comment on any video at [@theplacesnotfaces](https://www.youtube.com/@theplacesnotfaces).

I usually reply within a few days. I write in English and Spanish.

# What you can write to me about

- **Questions about a guide**, or help planning a trip to a place I've written about.
- **Corrections:** prices, opening hours and transport change often. If something in a guide is out of date, please tell me and I'll fix it.
- **Collaborations** with hotels, tour operators, tourism boards and travel brands. See [Work with me](/work-with-me/) for what I offer.
- **Press and photo licensing:** if you'd like to use one of my photos or videos, just ask.

# A couple of notes

- I can't make bookings for you. The booking links in my guides take you to the provider's own website, where you book directly.
- Some links on this site are affiliate links. You can read how that works in my [affiliate disclosure](/disclosure/), and how I handle data in the [privacy policy](/privacy/).
