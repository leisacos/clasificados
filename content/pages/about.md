Hi! I'm Laser, the person behind [@laser_2017_](https://www.instagram.com/laser_2017_/) on Instagram.

Instagram only has room for a photo and a caption. This blog is where I tell the whole story: how I got there, where I stayed, what it cost, and what I'd do differently next time.

# How I travel

- I book flights 4–8 weeks ahead and stay flexible on dates
- I mix budget stays with the odd splurge
- I never travel without insurance and an eSIM
