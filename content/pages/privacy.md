This site doesn't ask you to create an account and doesn't collect personal data directly. Your language choice is saved only in your own browser.

To understand which pages are useful, the site uses **Cloudflare Web Analytics**, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across other sites, and **Metricool**, a social media and web analytics tool that may set its own cookies to measure visits. The site also uses the **Google tag** from Google Ads, which may set cookies to measure how people who click my ads use the site and to show ads to past visitors. You can manage how Google personalises ads at [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com).

Affiliate partners (via Travelpayouts and Impact.com) and embedded Instagram posts and YouTube videos may set cookies to attribute bookings and display content. These cookies are covered by those providers' own privacy policies.

Questions? Send me a message on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/theplacesnotfaces/).
