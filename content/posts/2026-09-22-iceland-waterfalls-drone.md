Some places are big. **Iceland is huge**, and you only really feel it from the air. Waterfalls dropping off cliffs, black-sand rivers and green valleys that go on forever.

# Flying a drone in Iceland: what to know

- **National parks and many protected areas restrict drones.** Look for signs and check the rules for each site before you take off.
- Keep your distance from people and wildlife, and never fly over crowds at the popular waterfalls.
- Wind changes fast, so keep plenty of battery in reserve and bring the drone home early.

# A classic South Coast route

1. **Reykjavík** → the **Golden Circle** (Þingvellir, Geysir, Gullfoss)
2. **Seljalandsfoss** and **Skógafoss** waterfalls
3. **Reynisfjara** black-sand beach (stay well back from the waves)
4. **Jökulsárlón** glacier lagoon and Diamond Beach

# Getting there and around

International flights land at **Keflavík (KEF)**, about 45 minutes from Reykjavík. **Renting a car** is the best way to see the country at your own pace. Book a 4x4 if you're travelling in winter.

# Where to stay

Book early: accommodation along the Ring Road is limited and fills up fast in summer. Use **Reykjavík** as a base for the Golden Circle, then move along the coast (Vík, Höfn) to cut down on driving.

Hay lugares grandes. **Islandia es enorme**, y eso se siente de verdad desde el aire: cascadas que caen de acantilados, ríos de arena negra y valles verdes que no terminan nunca.

# Volar un drone en Islandia: lo que hay que saber

- **Los parques nacionales y muchas áreas protegidas restringen los drones.** Buscá los carteles y revisá las reglas de cada lugar antes de despegar.
- Mantené distancia de la gente y de los animales, y nunca vueles sobre multitudes en las cascadas más populares.
- El viento cambia rápido: guardá batería de reserva y traé el drone de vuelta con tiempo.

# Una ruta clásica por la Costa Sur

1. **Reikiavik** → el **Círculo Dorado** (Þingvellir, Geysir, Gullfoss)
2. Las cascadas **Seljalandsfoss** y **Skógafoss**
3. La playa de arena negra de **Reynisfjara** (mantenete lejos de las olas)
4. La laguna glaciar **Jökulsárlón** y la Diamond Beach
