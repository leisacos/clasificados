This fountain shoots water up to **150 m high**. The **Dubai Fountain** sits on Burj Lake, right under the Burj Khalifa, the tallest building in the world, and I filmed it from an abra boat on the lake. Would you take this boat ride?

# Dubai Fountain at a glance

- **What it is:** a choreographed water-and-light show set to music, on the artificial Burj Lake in Downtown Dubai.
- **How high:** the water goes up to around 150 m.
- **Cost:** watching from the waterfront promenade is generally free. The boat ride on the lake is a paid extra, so check current prices and times before you go.
- **When:** the shows run in the evening, and usually repeat through the night. Schedules change, so check the current timetable on the day.
- **Where:** next to the Dubai Mall and the base of the Burj Khalifa.

# Why watch it from a boat

Most people watch from the promenade or from a restaurant terrace, and both are good. The boat gives you something different: you are **on the lake itself**, with the water bursting up in front of you and the Burj Khalifa rising behind it. That is the view in my video. Traditional **abra** boats are the small wooden boats that also cross Dubai Creek, and some operators run them on Burj Lake as a short ride. Check which operator and which departure points are running now.

# How to get to the Dubai Fountain

- **Metro:** the Burj Khalifa/Dubai Mall station is the usual stop, and a covered walkway leads to the mall. Check the current route before you go.
- **Taxi or rideshare:** ask for the Dubai Mall, then walk out to the lake side.
- **On foot:** if you are staying in Downtown, the lake is a short walk.

# Tips for the best view

- **Arrive early** for a spot at the railing, especially on weekends and holidays.
- **Watch more than one show.** Each one uses a different song, so staying for a second show is worth it.
- **Film vertical, and start before the first burst.** The biggest jets can surprise you.
- **Evenings in summer are hot and humid.** Bring water, and note that cooler months are generally more comfortable outdoors.
- Boat rides depend on the weather and the operator's schedule, so keep a plan B on the promenade.

# Frequently asked questions

## Is the Dubai Fountain show free?
Watching from the public waterfront promenade is generally free. Boat rides on the lake and some restaurant seats are paid, so check the details before you go.

## How high does the Dubai Fountain go?
The jets reach up to about 150 m.

## What time is the Dubai Fountain show?
Shows run in the evening and repeat at set times. The timetable can change, so check the official schedule for the day you plan to visit.

## Where do you watch the Dubai Fountain from?
From the promenade by the Dubai Mall, from restaurants overlooking the lake, or from a boat on Burj Lake.
