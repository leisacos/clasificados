Lisbon was one of those cities that felt familiar from the first afternoon. I arrived late in the day, dropped my bag in Alfama and walked uphill until the city opened up below me.

# How to get to Lisbon

Lisbon Humberto Delgado Airport (LIS) is only 20 minutes from the centre by metro. Flights from most of Europe are cheap if you book 4–6 weeks ahead and stay flexible on dates.

# Where to stay

- **Alfama**: postcard streets and fado bars, but plenty of hills
- **Baixa / Chiado**: central and flat, best for a first visit
- **Príncipe Real**: quieter, leafy, great cafés

{{hotels}}

# What to do

1. Watch sunset from Miradouro da Senhora do Monte
2. Ride tram 28 early in the morning, before the queues
3. Take a day trip to Sintra and the Pena Palace
4. Eat pastéis de nata warm at Manteigaria
