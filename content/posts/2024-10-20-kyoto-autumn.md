Kyoto in late autumn is as beautiful as the photos, and just as busy. The trick is getting up before the tour buses.

# Day by day

1. Fushimi Inari at sunrise, then Tofuku-ji's maple valley
2. Arashiyama bamboo grove, Okochi Sanso villa and the river walk
3. Philosopher's Path, Eikan-do at night and dinner in Pontocho
