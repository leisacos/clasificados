# Getting there

Most visitors fly into Cancún (CUN), then take the ADO bus or rent a car down the coast. Tulum also has a newer airport (TQO) with a growing number of routes.

# My favourite cenotes

- **Gran Cenote**: easy to reach by bike from town
- **Dos Ojos**: incredible for snorkelling
- **Cenote Calavera**: small, quirky and fun to jump into
