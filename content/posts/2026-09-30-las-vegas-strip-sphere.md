From the lights of the **Strip** to the incredible visuals of the **Sphere**, Vegas is one of those places that feels almost unreal. And it's not all casinos: the city is packed with public art and installations that turn your world upside down, sometimes literally 😁

If Vegas is on your bucket list, this is your sign to start planning ✈️

# The Sphere

The giant LED globe lights up the skyline every night, and you can see the outside for free from the Strip. For the full experience, book a **show or immersive film inside**. Tickets sell out, so book ahead.

# Getting there

**Harry Reid International Airport (LAS)** is only a 10–15 minute ride from the Strip.

{{flights}}

# Best day trips from Las Vegas

- **Grand Canyon**: by road, small plane or helicopter
- **Hoover Dam**: under an hour away, and often combined with the Grand Canyon
- **Red Rock Canyon**: desert hikes and scenic drives just outside the city
- **Valley of Fire**: red sandstone that glows at sunset

# Where to stay

Mid-Strip hotels put you within walking distance of the main sights. **Downtown / Fremont Street** is cheaper and has a more old-school Vegas feel. Always check the **resort fee** when comparing prices.

# Cómo llegar

El **Aeropuerto Internacional Harry Reid (LAS)** queda a solo 10–15 minutos del Strip.

{{flights}}

# Las mejores excursiones desde Las Vegas

- **Gran Cañón**: en auto, avioneta o helicóptero
- **Represa Hoover**: a menos de una hora, y suele combinarse con el Gran Cañón
- **Red Rock Canyon**: caminatas en el desierto y rutas panorámicas a las afueras de la ciudad
- **Valley of Fire**: arenisca roja que brilla al atardecer

# Dónde alojarse

Los hoteles del centro del Strip te dejan a distancia caminable de lo principal. **Downtown / Fremont Street** es más barato y tiene un aire más de la Las Vegas clásica. Siempre revisá el **resort fee** al comparar precios.
